Institutional Policy on Course Allocation

Under BGNU academic regulations:

  • Teachers can only enroll students into courses formally allocated to them by the HOD.
  • The HOD provides the authorized student roster spreadsheet to each teacher.
  • Teachers submit their final graded marksheets back to the HOD for verification.
